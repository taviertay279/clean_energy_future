My Personal Action

My experience with renewable energy and sustainability in Singapore.

Challenges in Singapore

Limited Space

Singapore’s urban environment makes certain renewable energy sources difficult to implement, especially in smaller residential areas.

HDB Restrictions

Many HDB residents are unable to install personal renewable energy systems due to building limitations and regulations.

High Installation Costs

Renewable energy systems can be expensive initially, which discourages some families from switching to cleaner energy.

My Family’s Experience

Solar Panels at Home

Since my family lives in a landed property, we were able to install solar panels to generate renewable electricity.

Cleaner Energy Usage

Using solar energy reduced our reliance on fossil fuels and helped lower our environmental impact.

Long-Term Savings

Over time, renewable energy helped reduce electricity costs while supporting a cleaner environment.

Practical Tips for Homeowners

Use Energy Efficient Appliances

Switching to energy-saving appliances can significantly reduce electricity usage.

Reduce Electricity Waste

Turning off unused devices and lights helps conserve energy and lower utility bills.

Start Small

Homeowners can gradually adopt renewable energy systems over time instead of making immediate large investments.
