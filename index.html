Benefits of Clean Energy

Reduces Pollution

Renewable energy sources reduce harmful air pollution caused by fossil fuels.

Lowers Carbon Emissions

Cleaner energy helps slow climate change by reducing greenhouse gas emissions.

Improves Public Health

Cleaner air leads to healthier living conditions and fewer respiratory illnesses.

Types of Clean Energy

Solar Energy

Solar panels convert sunlight into electricity for homes and buildings.

Wind Energy

Wind turbines use wind power to produce clean electricity without pollution.

Hydroelectric Energy

Hydroelectric systems generate electricity using flowing water.

Which Energy Source Fits Your Home?

Landed Homes

Solar panels work best for landed homes because of larger roof space.

Coastal Homes

Wind energy is more effective in areas with stronger winds.

Rural Homes

Hydroelectric systems may benefit homes near rivers or water sources.

How to Convert Your Home to Clean Energy

1. Assess Energy Usage

Understand how much electricity your household consumes monthly.

2. Choose Renewable Energy

3. Install and Monitor

Install your system and monitor your electricity savings over time.
